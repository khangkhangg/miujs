Everyone may use, study, modify and share MiuJS for free under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE). In short:

- You can use it for any purpose, including commercial ones.
- If you distribute MiuJS or a modified version, **or run it as a network service** that people
  use, you must make the complete corresponding source code of your application available to
  those users under the AGPL-3.0.

Personal projects, open-source projects, education and research usually fit the AGPL with no
extra steps.

If you want to use MiuJS in a product or service **without** releasing your source code under
the AGPL, buy a commercial license. It gives you:

- The right to use and modify MiuJS in closed-source software and hosted services.
